Opportunities (Insights tab) are estimated savings with the calls that prove them: redundant reads, repeated failures, one-at-a-time lookups, bloated outputs, cache expiry, slow test loops, edit churn and repeated routines. Insights follow your selection, the whole session, or the visible timeline range.

Moments are inferred turning points: errors and how the agent recovered, shifts between exploring, building and verifying, unusually heavy thinking, and times you steered it mid-task.
